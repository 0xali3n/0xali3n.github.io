Technical interests

What I study and build toward

Interests grounded in work I have already shipped or studied — not a keyword list.

Full-stack software engineering

I care about owning vertical slices: interface clarity, API contracts, persistence, and the operational details that appear only after something is used. Internship and startup work both reinforced that “full-stack” is less a title and more a habit of following a feature across boundaries.

Applied artificial intelligence and generative AI

Through AdGenAI I worked with generative APIs as product infrastructure — prompts, model responses, storage, and review — rather than as isolated demos. I am interested in applied systems where models are components inside reliable workflows.

Blockchain and Web3 engineering

Hackathon and product work across Aptos, VeryChain, Supra, Algorand, and EVM-adjacent tooling taught me to treat wallets, gas, bridging, and explorer/debug surfaces as first-class product problems. I am interested in usable on-chain products, not only contract novelty.

Backend systems and architecture

Projects such as ZeroTrace pushed me toward backend clarity: state handling, encryption boundaries, and interfaces that can evolve without rewriting the product around them.

Developer tools and product engineering

Explorers, agent UIs, and claim flows are developer/user tools in disguise. I like products that reduce coordination cost for builders and operators.

Entrepreneurship and building technology products

Co-founding work at Anthé Labs made entrepreneurship concrete: team coordination, incubation context, roadmap tradeoffs, and the gap between a weekend prototype and something a user can trust. I remain interested in building companies around real problems.
