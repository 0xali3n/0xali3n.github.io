Project

Anthé Labs

Co-founder and product-builder work on a social trading and copy-trading product in the Aptos ecosystem.

AptosFull-stackProduct engineeringOn-chain integrations

What it is

Anthé Labs was a product effort focused on social trading and copy-trading workflows in the Aptos ecosystem. The work combined product design with full-stack and blockchain implementation.

Problem

Copy trading and social trading require trustworthy execution paths, readable risk signals, and interfaces that make following another trader's activity understandable — not just technically possible.

Why it was built

The product grew from hackathon prototyping into a registered startup effort incubated by Xyra Labs. The goal was to turn a constrained prototype into a coherent product with real on-chain infrastructure.

My contribution

  • Co-founded the company and worked within a five-person cross-functional team.
  • Contributed across product development, full-stack engineering, and blockchain integration.
  • Helped architect and ship on-chain infrastructure enabling copy trading, wallet execution flows, and live risk analytics surfaces.
  • Participated in end-to-end product lifecycle work, including roadmap thinking for advanced features such as Smart Grid & DCA bots and creator monetization concepts.

Technology

Aptos-focused on-chain infrastructure with accompanying full-stack product surfaces. Exact private implementation details and credentials are intentionally omitted.

Challenges and learnings

The hardest parts were less about writing a single feature and more about coordinating product, engineering, and go-to-market decisions while integrating blockchain execution into a usable trading experience.
