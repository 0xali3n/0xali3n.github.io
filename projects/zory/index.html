Project

Zory

Web3 social platform on VeryChain combining feed, quests, lottery, and swaps with sponsored gas interactions.

ReactTypeScriptVeryChainWepin SDKSupabaseSolidity

What it is

Zory is a social application on VeryChain with a feed, quests, a lottery, and swaps. Interactions are designed so users can participate without first topping up gas, because fees are sponsored.

Problem

Social and quest-style on-chain products often lose first-time users at the gas-funding step. Sponsored fees and a coherent product loop matter as much as contracts.

Why it was built

Built for the VERY Hackathon (January 2026) on VeryChain / DoraHacks.

My contribution

  • Built feed, quests, and token actions as one connected product flow.
  • Designed gasless-style interactions to improve first-time completion.
  • Kept reward accounting and token paths anchored to verified contracts.

Result

Track winner · $1,000 (as documented in portfolio competition records).
