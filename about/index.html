About

Chinmay Patil

Software engineer and product builder with a computer science foundation, hands-on experience shipping full-stack and blockchain products, and a current academic base in Germany.

I am Chinmay Chandragoud Patil — commonly Chinmay Patil, and also referenced as Chinmay Chandragoud. I build software products end-to-end: interfaces people can use, APIs and services that hold up under real workflows, and integrations into ecosystems where correctness and clarity matter.

My undergraduate degree is a B.E. in Computer Science from KLE Technological University (2021–2025). That period grounded me in algorithms, systems, data, and machine learning coursework, while most of my practical learning came from building — internships, startup work, and a long streak of hackathons.

Professionally, I have worked across full-stack engineering, applied AI product features, and blockchain development. At Anthé Labs, I co-founded and helped build a social trading / copy-trading product in the Aptos ecosystem with a five-person team, spanning product decisions, full-stack implementation, and on-chain integration. During my internship at Pravinya Infotech, I contributed to AdGenAI, an automated marketing-asset generator that combined a React frontend, Flask backend, and generative AI APIs.

Hackathons have been a consistent way I learn under constraints. Documented results include work across Aptos, VeryChain, Supra, Diamante, and ETHGlobal events, with multiple podium finishes. Those weekends produced projects such as Zory, W3Chat, and SupraScan — short-cycle builds that still taught lasting engineering habits.

I am currently based in Mainz, Germany, and enrolled in the M.Sc. Computer Science program at Technische Hochschule Bingen (TH Bingen). The master's is a deliberate next step: deepen software engineering and intelligent-systems foundations while continuing to build products that solve concrete problems.

I care about entrepreneurship as a practice of identifying real problems, shipping usable software, and iterating with users and collaborators — not as a slogan. If you are hiring, researching, organizing a hackathon, or looking for a co-builder, the contact page lists verified ways to reach me. For a visual project showcase, see my primary portfolio at chinmay.life.
