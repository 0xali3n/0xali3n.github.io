Background

Path into software and product work

How computer science study, product building, and competitive building environments shaped the work I do now.

Computer science foundation

I trained as a computer science engineer at KLE Technological University, completing a B.E. in Computer Science (2021–2025) with a CGPA of 7.41/10. Coursework emphasized machine learning, neural networks and deep learning, big data, exploratory data analysis, applied statistics (R), databases, data structures and algorithms, linear algebra, and calculus. The degree gave me vocabulary and structure; building shipped software taught me judgment.

Building as the main teacher

Alongside coursework, I spent significant time in team environments where the output had to work under time pressure — hackathons, internship delivery, and startup product cycles. That mix pushed me toward full-stack ownership: understanding how a UI decision becomes an API contract, how storage choices affect iteration speed, and how blockchain integrations introduce new failure modes.

Germany and the master's chapter

I now live in Mainz and study M.Sc. Computer Science at TH Bingen (from 2026). The move combines continued technical depth with proximity to European engineering and research communities. This site, together with chinmay.life, is meant to make that trajectory easy to verify and explore.
